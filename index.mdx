## Why commercial teams choose Cardinal

- A visible control point before an irreversible transaction.
- Explainable decisions that operations, risk and support teams can understand.
- Non-custodial integration: Cardinal never requests private keys or seed phrases.
- A controlled pilot route with agreed workflows and success measures.
- Clear boundaries alongside existing compliance, custody and legal controls.

<Warning>
  Cardinal does not replace KYC, KYB, AML, KYT, sanctions screening, source-of-funds review, custody, legal or title review, or licensed escrow. An ALLOW decision is not a safety guarantee; partner controls must also pass.
</Warning>
