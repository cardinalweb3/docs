## How Cardinal protects a transaction

Every protected transaction flows through the same three-step sequence before a signature is ever requested.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Evaluate intent">
    Your wallet, application, or integration submits the proposed transaction — sender, recipient, chain, token, and amount — to the Cardinal Protection API before prompting the user to sign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a decision">
    Cardinal's risk engine analyses network, recipient, wallet, and contract signals and returns a risk score, explainable findings, and a clear `ALLOW`, `REVIEW`, or `BLOCK` recommendation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route the transaction">
    Your application continues normally on `ALLOW`, surfaces the identified risk for explicit acknowledgement on `REVIEW`, or stops the flow entirely on `BLOCK` — before any value moves.
  </Step>
</Steps>
