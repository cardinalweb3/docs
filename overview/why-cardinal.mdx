## The problem buyers keep bringing us

Digital-asset platforms are held responsible for what their users sign, transfer, or settle, but the point of decision is opaque. A user approves a transaction they do not fully understand. An operations team sees the fraud only after value has moved. Compliance and support are left investigating outcomes instead of preventing them.

Cardinal moves that decision earlier. Before a wallet is asked to sign, before a withdrawal is broadcast, before a treasury signer approves, Cardinal evaluates intent and returns an explainable decision.

## What Cardinal is not

Cardinal is a transaction integrity and risk decisioning layer. It complements, and does not replace, the controls you already run.

<Note>
  Cardinal does not replace KYC, KYB, AML, KYT, sanctions screening, source-of-funds review, custody, legal or title review, or licensed escrow. Your existing controls remain in place; Cardinal adds an explainable decision point before value moves.
</Note>
