Cardinal is a working platform in active development. This page lists what is available today, what is in controlled pilot, and what is on the roadmap.

## Snapshot

| Capability | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protection API — pre-transaction risk decisioning | Available | Returns explainable ALLOW, REVIEW, BLOCK decisions before signing. |
| SafeSend — cancellable, time-locked transfers | Controlled testnet MVP | Currently on Arbitrum Sepolia testnet, available to design partners. |
| Programmable Escrow — protected settlement patterns | Final testing and security hardening | Available to selected design partners under pilot terms. |
| Enterprise Admin Dashboard | In development | Rolled out to design partners as capabilities land. |
| Wallet and exchange integrations | Ongoing | Delivered per pilot scope. |
| Incoming-funds tracing | Roadmap | Not available today. |
| Stolen/hacked-funds detection | Roadmap | Not available today. |

## What we do not claim

<Note>
  Cardinal does not replace KYC, KYB, AML, KYT, sanctions screening, source-of-funds review, custody, legal or title review, or licensed escrow. Cardinal is a transaction integrity and risk decisioning layer that complements your existing controls.
</Note>

- ALLOW is not a safety guarantee. Your other controls must also pass.
- Support and response commitments are agreement-specific. We do not publish fixed SLAs or 24/7 support commitments here.
- Commercial terms are agreed per pilot or deployment.
