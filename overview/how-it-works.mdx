Cardinal adds one explainable decision point to your existing transaction flow. It runs before signing or settlement, and returns a scored decision that your product can enforce, log and reconcile.

## The Check, Decide, Protect model

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check">
    Your platform sends transaction intent (counterparties, amount, context) to Cardinal's Protection API before requesting a signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Cardinal's Risk Engine returns a scored decision (ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK) with human-readable findings your teams can act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect">
    For high-value flows, transfers can be routed through SafeSend or Programmable Escrow to add a cancellation window or controlled release before funds are final.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where Cardinal fits

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Wallets">
    Cardinal is called before the wallet's sign prompt. Users see an explainable risk summary and your wallet enforces the decision.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Exchanges">
    Cardinal is called before an outbound withdrawal is signed or broadcast. Reviews are routed to your operations queue.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Custodians & Vaults">
    Cardinal is called before HSM or MPC signing. High-risk decisions route into your existing approval workflows.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="RWA & Payments">
    Cardinal is called before primary issuance, redemption, or large one-off payments. High-value flows can be routed through protected settlement.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## The three decision states

| Decision | What it means | Typical action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ALLOW` | Cardinal did not detect risk in the transaction intent. | Proceed subject to your other controls. |
| `REVIEW` | Cardinal identified findings that warrant review. | Route to operations, compliance, or additional user confirmation. |
| `BLOCK` | Cardinal identified findings that recommend blocking. | Prevent signing or settlement and surface the reason. |

<Note>
  ALLOW is not a safety guarantee. It means Cardinal did not detect risk on the checked signals. Your KYC, KYB, AML, sanctions and internal policy controls must still pass.
</Note>
