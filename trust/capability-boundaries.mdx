Cardinal is a transaction integrity and risk decisioning layer. This page is deliberately explicit about what Cardinal does and does not do today.

## What Cardinal is

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pre-transaction decisioning" icon="bolt">
    Explainable ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK decisions before signing or settlement.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Protected settlement patterns" icon="lock">
    Cancellable and controlled-release settlement rails for high-value flows.
  </Card>

  <Card title="An enterprise decision layer" icon="list-check">
    Auditable evidence that plugs into operations, risk and compliance workflows.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Non-custodial" icon="key">
    Cardinal never requests keys and never takes custody.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What Cardinal is not

<Note>
  Cardinal does not replace KYC, KYB, AML, KYT, sanctions screening, source-of-funds review, custody, legal or title review, or licensed escrow. These controls remain your responsibility.
</Note>

- Cardinal is not a licensed escrow provider.
- Cardinal is not a KYC, KYB or AML platform.
- Cardinal is not a sanctions or KYT screening provider.
- Cardinal is not a custody solution.
- Cardinal does not perform legal, title or source-of-funds review.

## On the roadmap

- Incoming-funds tracing.
- Stolen/hacked-funds detection.
- Additional network coverage, wallet and exchange integrations as pilots progress.

Roadmap items are not live and are not represented as available features.
