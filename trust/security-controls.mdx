Cardinal is designed to be a safe control point inside your existing stack. It never takes custody, never requests keys, and produces auditable evidence of every decision.

## Non-custodial and trust-minimised

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="No private keys, ever" icon="key">
    Cardinal never requests private keys or seed phrases from users, operators, or your platform.
  </Card>

  <Card title="No custody of user funds" icon="vault">
    Cardinal does not take custody of user assets. Your existing custody model is unchanged.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Explainable decisions

Every Protection API response includes findings your teams can read and act on. Decisions are not opaque scores; they are structured evidence for operations, compliance and support.

## Auditability

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Decision logs" icon="file-lines">
    Every request and outcome is logged to support investigations, reconciliation and reporting.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fits existing audit" icon="puzzle-piece">
    Cardinal produces evidence your existing audit, risk and compliance workflows can consume.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Enforcement discipline

<Note>
  ALLOW is not a safety guarantee. It means Cardinal did not detect risk on the signals it checked. Partner controls (KYC, KYB, AML, sanctions, internal policy) must also pass before value moves.
</Note>

## Where Cardinal does not go

Cardinal focuses on transaction integrity and pre-transaction decisioning. See [Capability Boundaries](/trust/capability-boundaries) for what Cardinal explicitly does not do.
