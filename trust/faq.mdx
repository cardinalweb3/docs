<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does Cardinal do, in one sentence?">
    Cardinal evaluates transaction intent before signing or settlement and returns an explainable decision your platform can enforce, log and audit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cardinal ever hold funds or keys?">
    No. Cardinal is non-custodial and trust-minimised. It never requests private keys or seed phrases and never takes custody of user assets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cardinal replace KYC, AML or sanctions screening?">
    No. Cardinal is a transaction integrity and risk decisioning layer. It complements, and does not replace, KYC, KYB, AML, KYT, sanctions screening, source-of-funds review, custody, legal or title review, or licensed escrow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is ALLOW a safety guarantee?">
    No. ALLOW means Cardinal did not detect risk on the signals it checked. Partner controls must also pass before value moves.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is available today versus on the roadmap?">
    The Protection API's pre-transaction decisioning is available. SafeSend is a controlled Arbitrum Sepolia testnet MVP. Programmable Escrow is in final testing and security hardening. Incoming-funds tracing and stolen/hacked-funds detection are roadmap only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a pilot take?">
    A typical Cardinal design-partner pilot runs for 60 to 90 days. Final scope, timelines and commercial terms are agreement-specific.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does a pilot cost?">
    Commercial terms are agreed per pilot or deployment. Cardinal does not publish standard pricing at this stage.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cardinal offer 24/7 support or an SLA?">
    Support and response commitments are agreement-specific. We do not publish 24/7 support or fixed SLAs on this site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which networks and assets are supported?">
    Coverage evolves per pilot and deployment. SafeSend's current MVP runs on Arbitrum Sepolia testnet. Speak to us about coverage for your specific flow.
  </Accordion>
