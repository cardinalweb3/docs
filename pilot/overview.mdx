Cardinal delivers new work with design partners through a controlled pilot. A pilot is a bounded engagement where we integrate Cardinal into a specific workflow, agree measurable outcomes, and validate commercial value before wider rollout.

## What a pilot is

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Scoped to one workflow" icon="crosshairs">
    A single, high-value flow (e.g. withdrawals, treasury transfers, RWA issuance) rather than a platform-wide rollout.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Measurable outcomes" icon="chart-line">
    Success measures agreed with your operations, product, risk and compliance teams before we start.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Controlled duration" icon="calendar-days">
    Typically 60 to 90 days from kickoff to review. Final scope, timelines and commercial terms are agreement-specific.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Shared learning" icon="handshake">
    Design partners help shape product decisions and get direct access to the Cardinal team.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Commercial terms are agreed per pilot or deployment. Cardinal does not publish standard pricing at this stage.
