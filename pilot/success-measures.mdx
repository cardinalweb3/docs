Pilots only work when both sides agree what "good" looks like up front. We work with each partner to pick a small number of measures that map to the business outcome your buyer already tracks.

## Categories we agree measures against

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fraud and risk" icon="shield-check">
    Volume, rate and pattern of risky transactions caught at the decision point.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Operational efficiency" icon="gauge-high">
    Time to review, dispute volumes, and reduction in reactive investigation load.
  </Card>

  <Card title="User and stakeholder trust" icon="handshake">
    Feedback from users, operations, compliance and risk teams working the flow.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Integration and reliability" icon="plug">
    Availability, latency, and integration stability under real traffic.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Example measures a partner might pick

| Category | Example measure |
| --- | --- |
| Fraud and risk | Number of transactions moved from ALLOW to REVIEW or BLOCK during the pilot window. |
| Operational | Time from decision to operational action on REVIEW outcomes. |
| Trust | Qualitative feedback from operations and compliance leads. |
| Reliability | Availability and latency of the decision point during the pilot window. |

<Note>
  Measures are illustrative. We do not commit to specific loss-reduction percentages or guaranteed outcomes. Every pilot agrees its own targets in writing.
</Note>
