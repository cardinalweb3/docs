## Add a safety window before final settlement

SafeSend is a protected transfer pattern for workflows where an immediate, irreversible transfer creates unnecessary risk. It introduces a defined time window in which an authorised action can cancel before settlement completes.

## How SafeSend supports the workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the protected transfer">
    The sender initiates a SafeSend transaction under the agreed pilot configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the time lock">
    Funds follow the configured smart-contract flow rather than settling immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor the safety window">
    The partner can apply its operational, legal and compliance checks during the defined period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle or cancel">
    The transfer completes when the conditions are met, or an authorised cancellation occurs under the agreed rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  SafeSend is a controlled MVP on the Arbitrum Sepolia testnet and is available to selected design partners under pilot terms. It is not represented as a production-ready mainnet service.

## Operating boundary

<Warning>
  SafeSend is not licensed escrow, custody, legal or title review, KYC, KYB, AML, KYT, sanctions screening or source-of-funds review. Cardinal is non-custodial and never requests or holds private keys or seed phrases.
</Warning>
