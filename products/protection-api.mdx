## Turn transaction risk into an action

The Protection API evaluates a proposed digital-asset transaction before it is signed or submitted. It gives the partner workflow an explainable decision:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="ALLOW" icon="circle-check">
    Continue only when Cardinal and the partner's own controls permit it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="REVIEW" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Pause for additional user confirmation or operational review.
  </Card>

  <Card title="BLOCK" icon="octagon-xmark">
    Stop the proposed transaction when the configured risk threshold is reached.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Commercial value

- Reduce avoidable losses caused by risky or erroneous transfers.
- Give operations and support teams a consistent decision trail.
- Introduce policy without taking custody of customer assets.
- Start with one defined workflow and measure the result during a controlled pilot.

## Operating boundary

<Warning>
  Cardinal is non-custodial and never requests private keys or seed phrases. The Protection API does not replace KYC, KYB, AML, KYT, sanctions screening, source-of-funds review, custody, legal or title review, or licensed escrow. ALLOW is not a safety guarantee; partner controls must also pass.
</Warning>
