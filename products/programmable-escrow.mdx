## What it does

Programmable Escrow is a protected settlement pattern for buyer/seller and multi-approver flows. Funds are held in a controlled state and released only when defined conditions or thresholds are met, giving both sides an auditable settlement moment.

## Operating boundary

<Warning>
  Programmable Escrow is not a substitute for licensed escrow services, legal or title review, custody, KYC, KYB, AML, sanctions or source-of-funds review. It is a protected settlement pattern that complements the controls already in place.
</Warning>
