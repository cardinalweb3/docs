Cardinal is the trust and protection layer for digital asset transactions. It sits between a composed transaction and the wallet signature request, evaluating risk across multiple signal layers and returning a clear, explainable decision — so your application can act before value moves rather than after a loss has occurred.

## The ALLOW / REVIEW / BLOCK decision model

Every call to the Cardinal Protection API returns one of three recommended actions. Your application routes the user based on that decision.

| Decision | Meaning | Application behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ALLOW` | Risk is within acceptable bounds | Continue to the protected signature or contract flow |
| `REVIEW` | One or more risk signals require user attention | Surface the findings and require explicit acknowledgement before continuing |
| `BLOCK` | Risk is high enough to stop the transaction | Halt the protected flow immediately — do not proceed |

The response also includes a numeric `risk_score`, a `risk_level` classification, and a `findings` array that explains what triggered the decision. Build your application logic from `recommended_action`, not from the score alone.

## The 5-layer risk engine

Cardinal evaluates each transaction across five independent signal layers before producing a decision.

1. **Network validation** — Confirms the target chain is active and the transaction is well-formed.
2. **Scam and fraud checks** — Cross-references recipient addresses and contract identifiers against known scam datasets and community threat signals.
3. **Wallet intelligence** — Analyses the behavioural and reputational history of both the sender and recipient wallets.
4. **Contract analysis** — Inspects contract verification status, permission scope, and known risk patterns for contract interactions.
5. **Simulation** — Models the expected on-chain outcome of the transaction to surface unexpected state changes or value drains before signing.

Each layer contributes to the final `risk_score`. The `findings` array in the response maps individual signals back to the layers that produced them, giving your users a clear explanation of the risk.

## Built for digital finance platforms

Cardinal is designed for teams that move digital asset value on behalf of users and need protection that operates at the point of intent — before a signature, not after a loss.

- **Wallets** — Protect every send, swap, and contract interaction before the user signs.
- **Exchanges and custodians** — Screen withdrawals and transfers before they leave the platform.
- **Payment platforms** — Validate recipient addresses and chain state before initiating settlement.
- **Marketplaces** — Evaluate contract interactions and token approvals before execution.
- **RWA and tokenisation platforms** — Verify on-chain recipients and contract conditions before asset transfer.
