## Buyer problem

Custodians and vault operators run tight approval workflows, but the decision at the point of signing is often binary and opaque. Compliance and risk teams want an explainable check that fits alongside HSM, MPC and existing policy engines, without giving up control of keys or workflow.

## Cardinal solution

Cardinal is called before the HSM or MPC signer is asked to sign. It returns a scored, explainable decision that plugs into your existing approval workflow and audit trail. Cardinal never touches keys and never takes custody.

## Example protected workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Signer or automated policy proposes a transaction">
    Your custody workflow assembles the transaction intent and prepares required approvers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your policy engine calls Cardinal">
    Before the HSM or MPC signer is engaged, the Protection API is called with the transaction intent and context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cardinal returns an explainable decision">
    ALLOW, REVIEW or BLOCK is returned with human-readable findings your risk and compliance teams can act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approvers enforce the outcome">
    ALLOW proceeds through your existing approval path. REVIEW and BLOCK route into compliance, risk or executive-approval queues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decisions and outcomes are logged">
    Every step is retained for internal audit, external reporting and post-incident review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Capability boundary

<Warning>
  Cardinal does not replace custody, KYC, KYB, AML, KYT, sanctions screening, source-of-funds review or licensed escrow. It adds an explainable decision layer before signing. ALLOW is not a safety guarantee; partner controls must also pass.
</Warning>
