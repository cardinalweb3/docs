## Buyer problem

Exchanges carry the reputational and financial cost of fraudulent withdrawals, hijacked accounts and social-engineering attacks. Reviews are often reactive, and support teams spend hours reconstructing what happened after value has already left the platform.

## Cardinal solution

Cardinal places an explainable risk decision point before an outbound withdrawal is signed or broadcast. Every withdrawal is evaluated for intent and counterparties, and returns a scored ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK decision that plugs into your operations queue and audit trail.

## Example protected workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="User requests a withdrawal">
    Your platform captures the withdrawal request and assembles the transaction intent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your backend calls Cardinal">
    Before signing or broadcast, your service calls the Protection API with counterparties, amount, asset and context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cardinal returns a scored decision">
    ALLOW, REVIEW or BLOCK is returned with human-readable findings your teams can act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your platform enforces the outcome">
    ALLOW proceeds subject to your other controls. REVIEW pauses the withdrawal into your operations queue. BLOCK stops the transfer with an explainable reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You log the decision for audit">
    Every decision, finding and downstream outcome is retained for investigations, reconciliation and reporting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
